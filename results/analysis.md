## Negative or weaker-than-expected results

1) The fixed-n binomial matches e-process raw power (303 vs 292 of 600, p=0.32). The e-process wins only on anytime validity and 35-59% fewer queries, because both tests saturate at 9 canaries and the plug-in bet pays a learning cost. 2) GA (0.010) and GradDiff (0.12+-0.13) are 'verified as deleted'. GA does this by collapsing the model (MMLU 0.27, about chance; TOFU-retain 0.18). GradDiff loses TOFU-retain accuracy 0.84->0.61 and genuine-fact accuracy 0.73->0.52. The BRIEF expected detection within 2-3 canaries; this is refuted. 3) RMU is a weak unlearner in our config (layer 7, lr 5e-5): 84% of deleted-canary ranks are unchanged vs planted and genuine-fact accuracy is unchanged (0.735 vs 0.73). Its 0.75 detection reflects ineffective unlearning, not verifier strength. 4) Prop 4 is vacuous: canaries are detectable (AUC>=0.998 even with held-out phrasing; the in-distribution slot types novella/early_award are still >=0.99), and adaptive suppression costs no genuine content. Detection of adaptive providers (0.45-0.56) comes from imperfect unlearning. 5) The pre-registered defaults are not the best post hoc: cloze-only probes and fixed top-1 betting dominate. 6) The single 7B NPO run evades detection (8/60) at a genuine-accuracy cost. It is a single seed; the 3B NPO spread is 26-46/60. 7) The Thm 2(i) top-1 bound is loose (8.5 vs ~3-4 canaries observed); only the KL form is predictive. 8) Sampled answers (E7, 20 canaries) are weak: only lazy at T=0.7 is significant (0.60 vs 0.10, Holm p=5e-7). Retrain at T=0.7 shows 0.30 (raw p=0.011, Holm 0.079). The planted string rarely appears (freq<=0.14), so text-only APIs would need many more queries. 9) The planting-rate effect is small and inconsistent across keys (trend p 0.002-0.65). 10) MIA is invalid, not merely weak (phantom FPR up to 0.45). 11) Held-out-detector thresholds transfer badly (TPR 0.0-0.34), and suppression from them was not run. 12) Null caveats: phantom canaries are shared across the models of a run, so per-run null rates correlate (m+1=2 phantom top-1 is 0.37-0.44 on key0 Qwen vs 0.57-0.64 on key1). key2 retrain chi-square p=0.042 is 1 of 2 raw rejections among 37 tests. Retrain phantoms 6/60 come from 2 subjects. 13) Inherited from GAPS O1-O14: Llama and 7B single seed, no ROUGE-L, RAG compliant without phantoms and single key, no retained probes on GradDiff/RMU/adaptive, adaptive runs from one flagged set, memorisation buckets per canary. 14) results/analysis.md could not be written (agent harness blocks report .md files); the content is in this stage-result JSON.
